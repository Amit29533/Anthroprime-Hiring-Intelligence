# Stage 4: candidate and account feedback loops

Implemented locally for continuation milestone 4 in the [five-phase audit](FIVE_PHASE_IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT_2026_10_07.md#next-build-order). This is the existing-stack feedback milestone, not the similarly numbered roadmap stage for additional services. Nothing has been pushed, deployed, sent externally or applied to a hosted database.

## What is implemented

| Workflow | Behavior and access |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate account grants | An administrator records the verified registered Auth user UUID, review reason and expiry within 30 days. Existing Auth signs in the recipient. Grants can be revoked and renewed, and every read/write checks the current grant and root identity. Grants do not create users or send invitations. |
| Candidate account selection | The portal lists at most 100 explicitly granted current identities. Email matching no longer grants access. Multiple accounts use an explicit chooser; the legacy status overview fails closed when its implicit selection would be ambiguous. |
| Reviewed profile proposals | Candidates propose name, title, company, location, summary, total/relevant experience, notice, earliest start, availability status, mode, engagement and preferred locations. Blank numeric/date fields can represent unknown; zero notice is known. Candidate assertions remain pending until an independent administrator/recruiter accepts or rejects them with a reason. |
| Alternate-contact proposals | Candidates can supply a new alternate email or phone. Acceptance uses the existing contact validation, duplicate checks, limits, receipts and provenance ledger. It creates an unverified contact and does not replace primary contact fields, link another Auth user or renew contact verification. Duplicate contacts abort the entire acceptance transaction. |
| Proposal conflict/recovery | A keyed baseline over the curated profile and a separate keyed proposal review head prevent stale acceptance and double review. Exact operation receipts survive lost acknowledgements. A changed profile requires a fresh candidate proposal; the reviewer can reject the obsolete proposal. Candidates can withdraw their own pending proposals. Retired proposals remain reviewable history under the survivor; they cannot be applied to the survivor. |
| Immediate purpose preferences | Candidates record explicit recruiting-contact/marketing opt-outs and UTC quiet windows. These append to the Stage 3 preference journal and immediately affect eligibility. Removing a preference opt-out does not grant or reinstate consent. Preferences remain available under a processing hold. |
| Freshness invitations | A reviewer previews the exact question, recipient, scope and expiry, then confirms a manually shared authenticated link. Responses record assertions/intent and do not mark a profile verified. |
| Redeployment invitations | The placement review queue discovers active/completed placements with a recorded end date in the previous 180 days or next 30 days. Issuance additionally requires an open demand, current purpose consent/preferences, an unrestricted current candidate and a grant covering the invitation expiry. Interest records a response; it does not create a placement, submission or assessed-ready decision. |
| Candidate and account surveys | A reviewed frozen question (10–500 characters) accepts a 1–5 rating and bounded comment. Candidate surveys require recruiting-contact eligibility. Client surveys use the existing client memberships, including demand-limited scopes. No candidate profile/contact/compensation payload is added to client survey responses. |
| Scoped manual links | `/portal.html#request=<UUID>` and `/client.html#request=<UUID>` resolve the exact invitation through Auth and current permissions, select the authorized account and filter to that invitation even beyond the first history page. A UUID is a locator, not a bearer credential. Expired/cancelled/responded history remains readable only while the underlying account access permits it. |
| Account/recruiter review | Candidate 360 has **Feedback & self-updates**. Client detail has **Account feedback review**. Both show reasons, proposal comparisons, invitation state and pending/actioned/dismissed responses. Survey totals, averages and pending counts cover the selected identity family/client, not only the visible page. |
| Workspace queue | Repository overview provides bounded pending-proposal, pending-response and placement-end review queues, with candidate/client navigation. Placement dates identify review opportunities; they are not proof of consent, readiness or verified availability. |

All new private tables have RLS and no raw authenticated/anonymous table privileges. Public RPC wrappers are invokers; guarded private entry points perform authoritative current membership/grant checks under an empty search path. Viewer access is read-only; assessor/sales assignments do not confer full feedback journal administration. Full administrator/recruiter roles review; administrators grant/revoke candidate access with existing opt-in privileged MFA. Client members answer only their recipient- and demand-scoped invitations. Anonymous callers, arbitrary recipients and other tenants are denied.

## Integrity and limits

Writes share the workspace → candidate → journal lock order. Candidate grants and client memberships are checked and share-locked during self-service. A changed/revoked grant, narrowed client membership, removed issuing reviewer, expired link, retired identity, processing hold, revoked/future consent, opt-out, profile change, demand change or placement change prevents an eligible response. Open stale/suppressed invitations are labelled accordingly and require cancellation/reissue; contents are not silently refreshed. Exactly one response is stored per invitation. Review disposition does not automatically change pipeline or placement state.

Profile acceptance uses the original identity and baseline; it preserves the recorded verification date and existing financial boundaries. The portal submits only changed fields. Contexts exceeding 16,000 bytes fail closed for internal correction; profile records are not silently truncated. Existing profile/contact history and downstream source invalidation remain in force. Compensation, assessed skills/readiness, internal notes, consent grants, verification claims and identity reassignment are not self-editable fields. Proposal decision reasons are visible to the candidate; the reviewer screen makes that explicit. The candidate portal also provides sign-out and ignores stale account responses after a session change.

History and queues use 25-row pages; demand context search returns at most 100 matches. Recipient catalogs are bounded (candidate grants use paged history; client memberships already cap 50 scopes). Pending proposals cap at five per candidate; new proposals and invitations cap at 20 per candidate/client per rolling 24 hours. Candidate preferences cap at 20 observations per actor/candidate per 24 hours. Subjects/comments/reasons and request byte sizes are bounded. Survey aggregation is scoped but still scans its matching ledger; bounded payloads do not prove constant-time database work. Offset paging is a live operational view and can shift after concurrent insertions; refresh before acting.

Lost acknowledgements retain the exact operation UUID, scope, head, expiry and payload. The UI disables dependent edits and provides explicit retry/discard. Discarding a local retry draft does not undo a server write; refresh history before creating another request. Candidate/account switches discard local drafts and ignore late results. Refresh errors clear the current projection instead of silently retaining a successful-looking page.

No new Netlify function, environment variable, paid provider, scheduler or worker is required. Stage 3 retains 18 packaged functions and test-only transport. Links are manually copied/shared; no email/SMS/provider delivery, anonymous token link, HTML email, mailbox, OAuth or live-calendar dependency is introduced. Surveys are fixed rating/comment workflows, not an arbitrary public survey builder.

## Privacy coverage

The second migration adds five D7 inventory categories: feedback grants, proposals, prompts, responses and receipts. The current inventory has **57 categories**, including candidate-family records retained after merges. Account-only commercial survey records are not automatically classified as candidate records. D6 explicitly excludes these private records pending separate approved disclosure review. Candidate preference observations already belong to the Stage 3 category. Recapture D7 reviews and regenerate reviewed D6 packages. No erasure, consent grant or legal-fulfillment assertion occurs.
